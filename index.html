About the Club

The Orange Ave club sits inside the SoDo Shopping Center at 15 W Crystal Lake St — minutes from downtown and Orlando Health. Doors open at 5 AM on weekdays, built for commuters who train before the shift and for anyone who wants a coach in their corner after it.

It's a full-service club in the truest sense: an indoor lap pool, a full basketball court, complete strength and cardio floors, a 16-class weekly schedule, four Master Trainers, and a sauna-steam-whirlpool recovery suite to finish.

Good Questions

Before You Come In

What are the club's hours?

Mon–Thu 5:00 AM–11:00 PM, Fri 5:00 AM–9:00 PM, Sat–Sun 8:00 AM–7:00 PM. Thanksgiving Day (Thu, Nov 26, 2026) runs special hours: 8:00 AM–12:00 PM. The status pill at the top of this page always shows whether the club is open right now.

How does the free guest pass work?

It's a free 3-day pass for you and a friend, for non-members. Activate it within 30 days of issue; once activated it's good for 3 consecutive days. One pass per person every six months. Request it on the official pass page, pick one up at the club, or call (321) 732-6018. The club's full official terms are on its pass page.

How old do you have to be to use a guest pass?

16 or older on your own — or 13+ when accompanied by a parent or legal guardian, who signs the under-18 waiver. Bring a current picture ID with your birth date either way.

Are group classes included with membership?

Yes — the club lists group fitness classes as included at no additional charge. That's all 16 weekly sessions across Zumba®, Yoga, Mat Pilates, Aqua Fit, Body Works Plus Abs and Zumba Gold®.

What is the complimentary fitness assessment?

It's the free starting point for personal training: a certified assessment of your fitness level, a body composition test if you want one, a personalized goal timeline, an equipment overview, and a quick one-on-one workout. 18+ with valid ID, one per person — call ahead to schedule.

Does personal training cost extra?

Yes — the club lists personal training with an asterisk: it's a separate agreement at an additional charge, and rates are confirmed at the club. The fitness assessment that starts it is complimentary.

Is Kids Klub open?

Not right now — this club lists Kids Klub closed Monday through Sunday. It's LA Fitness's in-club babysitting service where offered. Call the club for the latest before planning a visit around it.

Where do I park?

The club sits inside the SoDo Shopping Center at 15 W Crystal Lake St, just south of downtown off S Orange Ave, with surface parking at the center. Tap Get Directions in the Visit section below and your maps app handles the rest.

What should I bring on my first visit?

A current picture ID with your birth date and workout gear. Guest-pass visits start with a signed waiver plus a fitness analysis and orientation with a membership counselor — then the club is yours: floor, class, pool, sauna.

I'm already a member — where's my account?

Member accounts live on LA Fitness's own systems, not on this concept page. Manage your membership at lafitness.com or in the LA Fitness mobile app — Pro Results clients also get Studio Zone® there.
